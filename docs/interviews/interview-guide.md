# Testora — user interview kit (section 2 "Evidence of relevance")

Goal: 3–5 short conversations (10–15 min) with people who are preparing for IELTS or took it recently.
Split: Rakhat — 2–3 people, Nurhan — 2–3 people. Telegram call, voice or text chat are all fine.
Record honestly: write down what the person actually said. If someone disagrees with our idea, write that too — the committee checks that verified findings are separated from assumptions.

## Before you start (say this)
"I'm doing a diploma project — an app that helps people prepare for IELTS. I'd like to ask about your experience, about 10 minutes. I won't publish your name — OK if I take notes?"

## Questions
1. When did you take / are you planning to take IELTS? What band did you need, and what did you get (if taken)?
2. How did you prepare? (courses, tutor, YouTube, books, apps — which ones?)
3. How much did you spend on preparation (approx.)? Was it affordable?
4. Which section was the hardest for you: Reading, Listening, Writing or Speaking? Why?
5. For Writing and Speaking — how did you find out what your band was and what your mistakes were? Who checked you?
6. Did you understand *why* your score was not growing? What was missing?
7. What would an ideal practice tool do for you? (open question — let them talk)
8. Show testora.studio (or describe it): "The app grades your essay by the 4 IELTS criteria, finds repeated mistakes, names the main reason your band is stuck and gives a task for today." — Would you use it? What would make you trust an AI band score?
9. How often would you practice with such a tool? Would you pay for it, and how much?
10. Anything else that annoyed you during preparation?
