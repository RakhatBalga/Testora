# 2. Evidence of Relevance

Requirement: 3–5 discussions with user representatives; distinguish verified findings from assumptions.

## 2.1 Method
- **Participants:** 3–5 people who are preparing for IELTS or took it in the last two years (recruited among students and acquaintances of the team).
- **Format:** semi-structured interview, 10–15 minutes, call or chat; notes taken with the participant's consent; no full names stored.
- **Interviewers:** Rakhat Balgabekov, Nurhan Turganbek.
- **Guide (10 questions):** exam goal and target band; how they prepared and how much it cost; hardest section; how they got feedback on Writing/Speaking; whether they understood why the score did not grow; ideal practice tool; reaction to the Testora loop; what would make an AI band score trustworthy; willingness to use/pay; other pain points. Full guide: `docs/interviews/interview-guide.md`.
